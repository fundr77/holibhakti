# holibhakti

Bhakti Health and wellness: 7D Wellness Health Coaching Platform

## Project Overview
Design a modern, professional health and wellness coaching website that supports multiple clients with a holistic approach to wellness. The site should be calming, trustworthy, and emphasize personalized, science-backed coaching services.

## Core Features Required

### 1. **Homepage**
- Hero section with impactful headline, short introductory text, and call-to-action
- Professional image or video showcasing wellness transformation
- Clear value proposition highlighting the 7D wellness approach
- Trust indicators (testimonials preview, credentials)

### 2. **About Section**
- Coach's story and philosophy
- Certifications and experience
- Holistic approach explanation (personalized, science-backed)
- Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

### 3. **7D Wellness Services & Programs**
Display each of the 7 dimensions:
- Physical Wellness
- Nutritional Wellness
- Mental/Emotional Wellness
- Mindfulness/Spiritual Wellness
- Gut Health
- Stress Management
- Sleep & Recovery

Each dimension should include:
- Brief description
- Benefits
- Pricing plan options with inquiry form

### 4. **Client Onboarding Flow**
**Discovery Call Process:**
- Book discovery call via website → auto WhatsApp welcome message
- After payment → WhatsApp sends onboarding checklist + client form

**Communication Schedule:**
- Weekly: Motivation nudges & progress reminders
- Monthly: Renewal reminders & testimonial requests

### 5. **Payment & Subscription System**
- Payment gateway integration (UPI, Cards) with GST registration/lengthy compliance
- Subscription options:
  - Monthly coaching programs
  - One-time workshops
  - Annual wellness plans
  - Group memberships (3+ members)
- Automated invoice & receipt generation

### 6. **Client Portal/Dashboard** (For Multiple Clients)
Secure login with access to:
- Personalized diet/exercise plans
- Progress tracking tools (weight, mood, sleep, habits)
- Session notes & recordings
- Chat/messaging for quick updates
- Resource library access (based on membership tier)

### 7. **Booking & Scheduling System**
- Integrated calendar with call option
- View available slots
- Book sessions (paid/free)
- Automatic reminders sent to clients
- WhatsApp notification integration

### 8. **Blog/Knowledge Hub**
Regular posts covering:
- Nutrition tips
- Fitness guidance
- Mindfulness practices
- Gut health information
- Stress management techniques

### 9. **Testimonials & Success Stories**
- Written testimonials with before/after health transformations
- Video testimonials (if available)
- Case studies showcasing real results

### 10. **Contact & Support**
- WhatsApp/chatbot integration
- Simple contact form
- Email support
- FAQ section

### 11. **Resource Center**
Tiered access system:
- **Free (Visitors)**: Basic e-notes, blog articles
- **Registered Users**: Diet charts, exercise plans, meditation audios
- **Paid Members**: Recorded webinars, advanced tools, progress tracking software

### 12. **Technology & Tools Integration**
- **CRM**: HubSpot, Zoho, or Practice Better for client relationship management
- **Email Automation**: Send reminders, motivation, progress check-ins
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Track client progress and business growth
- **WhatsApp Business API**: Automated messaging

## Design Requirements

### Visual Style
- Clean, modern, calming aesthetic
- Color palette: Soft greens, blues, earth tones (wellness-focused)
- High-quality images of healthy lifestyle, nature, wellness activities
- Ample white space for easy reading
- Mobile-responsive design

### User Experience
- Intuitive navigation
- Fast loading times
- Clear CTAs throughout
- Accessibility compliant
- GDPR/privacy compliant data handling

### Branding Elements
- Professional logo emphasizing "7D Wellness"
- Consistent typography
- Trust badges and certifications displayed
- Professional photography or illustrations

## Technical Specifications
- Platform: WordPress/Custom/Webflow (specify preference)
- SSL certificate for security
- SEO optimized
- WhatsApp integration
- Payment gateway integration
- Email marketing integration
- Analytics tracking (Google Analytics)
- Booking system plugin/integration

## Content Tone
- Empathetic and supportive
- Professional yet approachable
- Educational and empowering
- Action-oriented
- Evidence-based

## Success Metrics to Track
- Conversion rate (visitors to discovery calls)
- Client retention rate
- Engagement with resources
- Booking completion rate
- Subscription renewals
